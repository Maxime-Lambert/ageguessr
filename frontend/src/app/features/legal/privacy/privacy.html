Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }} ·

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the publisher of AgeGuessr, a private individual (see the Legal notice). For any question or request about your data: contact@ageguessr.app.

2. Playing without an account

We do not ask for anything. Like any website, your IP address and the technical details of your requests pass through our network provider and our server to deliver the pages; our server does not keep access logs. Your theme choice (dark or light) and the language of the hints are stored on your device only and never sent to us. The language of the site you choose is kept in a cookie, so that our server opens the site in that language.

When you play, your first guess gives your browser a random identifier, in the ageguessr_guest cookie. It tells us nothing about you; it only ties your guesses of the day together, so that you can reload the page and pick up where you were, and play each photo once (legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f): the game cannot work otherwise). We record your guesses, the hint you chose and your points against it. Your IP address also goes into an anti-abuse counter on guesses.

The free investigation and the challenges work the same way: starting a series or taking up a challenge gives your browser the same identifier. To share a series as a challenge, or to take one up, without an account, you choose a name for that challenge (3 to 20 letters, digits, "_" or "-"), which cannot be the username of an account. Everyone with the link to a challenge you shared sees your name as its sender; once they have played, they see it next to your score in the leaderboard: do not use your real name if you do not want to.

From the result of a round you played, you can report its photo or its hint, choosing a reason from a list. The report keeps only what is reported, the reason and the date: nothing about you. To count one report per round, the round you played remembers that you reported it, and is deleted with it. Your IP address goes into an anti-abuse counter on reports.

3. With an account: what we process and why

  • To provide your account (performance of the contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)): your email address, your username, your password (stored only as a bcrypt hash: we never see it), whether your email is confirmed, your account's creation date, your language (English or French: the site opens in it and our emails are written in it), and session and security tokens (stored hashed).
  • To send you service emails (same basis): confirming your address, resetting your password, securing or deleting your account. We send no newsletter and no advertising.
  • To protect accounts and the Service (legitimate interest, art. 6(1)(f)): the number of failed sign-in attempts and a temporary lock after too many, and, on sign-in and account requests, your IP address in an anti-abuse counter.
  • To prove you accepted the Terms (legitimate interest): the version of the Terms you accepted and when.
  • To keep your games (performance of the contract): your guesses, the hints you chose and your points, for each day you play while signed in, and for the free series and the challenges you play.

Your username is shown, with your score, to the players of a challenge you create or take up. Your email address is never shown.

Providing an email address and choosing a username are required to create an account: without them, no account can be created, but you can still play without one.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, including tokens.
  • Tokens expire on their own: 90 days for staying signed in, 1 to 24 hours for links sent by email.
  • Games played while signed in: with your account, deleted with it. Games played without an account: 7 days after the day played.
  • Free series: 7 days after they were started, unless shared. Challenges, with their leaderboard and the names chosen by the players without an account: 30 days after being shared. Deleting your account deletes the series you started, the challenges made from them, and your place in the challenges of others.
  • IP address in the anti-abuse counter: 1 minute, 1 hour for starting series, taking up challenges and reporting.
  • Reports of a photo or a hint: as long as the photo or the hint exists. They hold nothing about the player who sent them.
  • Emails at our email provider: 30 days.

5. Who else processes it

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use any audience measurement tool.

  • OVH SAS (France): hosting of the server and database, in the Gravelines data centre, France.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States): domain name system, delivery and protection of the site. It processes your IP address and request data.
  • Resend (United States): sending of service emails. It processes your email address and the content of these emails, kept for 30 days.

Transfers to the United States are covered by these providers' certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and local storage

  • ageguessr_refresh_token (cookie, 90 days): keeps you signed in. Not readable by scripts.
  • ageguessr_guest (cookie, 30 days): the random identifier of your games when you play without an account. Not readable by scripts, sent only to the game.
  • ageguessr_lang (cookie, 1 year): remembers the language of the site you chose, so that our server opens the site in it.
  • ageguessr-theme (local storage): remembers your theme choice.
  • ageguessr-hint-language (local storage): remembers the language of the hints you chose.
  • Cloudflare may set security cookies (such as __cf_bm) to tell humans from bots.

All of them are strictly necessary or store a choice you made, so they do not require your consent. There is no analytics or advertising cookie, hence no cookie banner.

7. Your rights

You can access your data, have it corrected or erased, receive it in a portable format, object to its processing based on legitimate interest, and ask for its processing to be restricted. You can change your username and delete your account yourself from your account page; for everything else, write to contact@ageguessr.app from the address of your account. We answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or with the data protection authority of your country.

8. People shown in the game

The game shows photos of public figures. Their name, dates of birth and death, photos, gender and the continent of their nationality are collected from publicly accessible sources, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons, where they are published under free licences; we do not get them from the people themselves. We keep only what the game needs, for as long as the game exists, and use it for nothing else: gender and continent serve only to balance the selection between women and men and between continents. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering a game about public figures: it concerns data these people have made public as part of their public life, already freely published, and we use none of it to profile, contact or rate them. We never show a photo taken before the person turned 18, even if they are an adult today.

So that the game does not feature people associated with grave violence or extremism, we also check public Wikidata records (convictions or charges for serious crimes, membership of far-right parties, heads of state or government of authoritarian states) solely to leave these people out of the game. We do not keep these records: we only note that the person was left out under our editorial line, without the reason. These are facts the people concerned made public or that are part of the public record, and we use them only to show nothing about these people.

Hints may also name public figures, for instance through their year of birth. These names and facts come from Wikipedia and Wikidata and are stored with the hint, on the same legal basis and for no other purpose. A hint built on someone's birth only names a person who was already an adult when it was written.

If you appear in the game or in a hint and want to be removed, or want a photo of you withdrawn, write to contact@ageguessr.app. We will delete your photos, withdraw the hints that name you, keep only what is needed to make sure you are never added again, and confirm it to you within one month.

9. Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and tokens are only stored hashed. Sign-in attempts are limited, and an account is temporarily locked after repeated failures.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top changes accordingly. If you have an account, you will be told of any significant change.
